02 · Markdown to PDF

Each mark turns into print

Morph: the Markdown is typed, then every line slides and grows into its printed form: # becomes the heading, > [!note] the teal callout, [x] the checkbox. Printer: the page feeds out of a printer slot instead.

One tap to PDF.

Export with one tap, choose from 4 PDF themes, and read the result in the built-in viewer.

03 · Arabic

The dot writes like a pen

The orange dot is the pen tip. It writes the letters from right to left, lifts off, then the vowel marks (diacritics) drop into place.

Arabic stays Arabic.

Right-to-left order, diacritics and joined letters are kept in the PDF.
