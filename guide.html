OwlMD Guide

A short tour of what OwlMD does today. Everything here exists in this version.

الدليل بالعربية · Guide for version 1

Create a note

Tap the + button in the middle of the floating bottom navigation bar. It is there on every tab (Documents, Search, PDFs, Settings). A new note opens ready to type, inside the folder you have open (from other tabs it goes to the top level). Your text saves itself; a small dot at the top of the editor shows the state. When you reopen the app, a Continue card on the Documents screen can take you back to the note you last edited.

Tap the note's title at the top of the editor to rename it.

In a folder with no notes yet, the New note button in the middle of the screen starts a note in that folder.

The editor toolbar

The row above the keyboard inserts Markdown for you:

  • Bold, Italic, Heading, Bullet list, Checkbox, Numbered list, Quote, Link, Code, Highlight.
  • Undo and Redo.
  • Indent and Unindent appear when the cursor is in a list.
  • Insert (the ⊕ button at the end) opens a sheet with Image, Table, Callout, Code block, Divider, Page break, Link and Footnote.

The Link button opens a form with the text and the URL. Put the cursor inside a link and tap it again to edit it, or use Remove link to keep the text and drop the link.

The Image button offers four sources: Gallery, Camera, URL and In app (pictures you already used). Add alt text and a caption, choose a size (Fill width, Original, Large, Medium) and an alignment. Pictures you pick or take are copied into OwlMD and resized. To change one later, put the cursor inside it or tap the picture in the preview, then tap the Image button.

Preview

On a phone, swipe to move between the editor and the live preview. On a wide screen the two sit side by side, and the floating button at the top hides the editor. Web and email links in the preview open in your browser or mail app.

The ⋮ (More actions) menu in the editor's top bar has Choose PDF theme, Share as Markdown, Jump to heading and Right-to-left document.

Callouts

A callout is a quote block with a type tag:

Callouts

Write > [!type] Title and put the text on the next lines that start with >.

The 14 types are: note, abstract, info, todo, tip, important, success, question, warning, failure, danger, bug, example and quote. Each has its own colour and icon, in the preview and in the PDF. The Callout tile in the Insert sheet lets you pick the type.

Tables

Tap Insert, then Table, to add a small table:

Cell Cell

Edit the cells and add rows by typing more lines that start with |. Tables show in the preview and in the PDF.

Arabic and English in one note

You can mix Arabic and English freely, and write Western digits (0-9). By default each paragraph, heading and list item takes the direction of its first strong letter, and a block with no letters follows the one before it. Code blocks stay left to right.

To make the whole note right to left, open ⋮ → Right-to-left document. The choice is saved per note and also applies to the PDF. The app's own language is chosen in Settings → Appearance and language → Language.

Folders

Every row in the Documents list has the same shape: the title (up to two lines, folders in bold), then a small line with the date and the reading time, such as "Today 10:12 · 3 min". Today's notes show the time; older ones show the date.

  • New folder (the folder button in the Documents top bar) asks for a name. Folders nest up to 3 levels deep; a folder's ⋮ menu has New subfolder, Rename and Delete.
  • A note's ⋮ menu has Move to folder, Tags, Rename and Delete. No folder (root) moves it back to the top level.
  • The name boxes (new folder, rename folder, rename note) are the same: the button stays off until you type something, and a name that another folder or note in the same place already uses shows a message instead of saving. Pressing Enter on the keyboard confirms.
  • When you make a copy of a note or a PDF, or restore one from Trash into a place where the name is taken, the copy is called "Name (2)", "Name (3)" and so on.
  • Swipe a row toward the start of the line (right to left in English, mirrored in Arabic) to reveal its actions. A note shows Move to folder, Tags and Delete. A folder shows Rename and Delete. A long swipe asks you to confirm the delete. A note deleted from its ⋮ menu goes to Trash straight away, with Undo.
  • Press and hold a note to select several at once, then tag, move or delete them together.
  • The Tags button above the list filters by tag. Manage all tags under Settings → Reading and library → Manage tags.
  • The sort control orders by Last modified, Name or Length.

Trash

Deleted notes, folders and PDFs go to Trash and stay there for 7 days, then are removed for good. Open Trash from Settings → Reading and library → Trash or from the Documents ⋮ → Trash. Tap Restore to bring an item back (a folder returns with what was deleted in it). Delete permanently removes an item at once, and Empty Trash clears everything.

PDF export and themes

Tap the PDF button in the editor's top bar. Choose a file name, tick Printable if you want black and white with no tinted fills, and tap Export. The PDF opens in the share sheet and is kept in the PDFs tab.

Pick the look under ⋮ → Choose PDF theme. There are four themes: Professional, Modern, Minimalist and Standard. Each shows a sample page made by the real export engine, with the callouts drawn the way they will print. Tap a preview to enlarge it. A line <!-- pagebreak --> (or the Page break tile) starts a new PDF page.

The PDFs tab lists every export with its date, page count and size. The All folders chip above the list shows the PDFs of one folder only (the choice lasts until you close the app). Sort by date, name or folder, share one file, or use Share all as zip.

Search

The Search tab looks in titles and note text as you type. Title only limits it to titles. Recent searches are kept below the field.

Bring notes in and share them out

  • Documents ⋮ → Open file copies a .md, .markdown or text file (up to 5 MB) into OwlMD. The original is never changed.
  • Documents ⋮ → Import from GitHub loads a Markdown file from a GitHub file link.
  • You can share text or Markdown files from other apps into OwlMD (Android).
  • ⋮ → Share as Markdown shares the note's .md file. Pictures stored inside OwlMD do not travel inside it, and you see a warning. PDFs are not affected.

Open Markdown files with OwlMD

  • Tap a .md or .markdown file in Files, Drive, an email or a chat, choose OwlMD, then Always to make it the default.
  • If another app opens it instead: in your phone's Settings → Apps → that app → Open by default, clear the defaults (the names may differ on your phone), then tap the file again.
  • OwlMD opens a copy as a new note; the original file is not changed. Opening the same file again makes another copy.
  • Files over 5 MB, and files that are not text, are refused with a message.

Backup and restore

Open Settings → Privacy and data → Backup. A sheet offers three actions:

  • Save backup to a file saves a .zip file to a place you choose. Send backup sends the same file through the share sheet.
  • The file contains your notes (including those in Trash), folders, tags and the pictures your notes use. It does not contain settings or exported PDFs.
  • Restore from a backup replaces every note, folder and tag in the app with the file's contents. It asks you first, and says it cannot be undone. Pictures are restored too.
  • OwlMD can still read an older .json backup, but not one larger than 150 MB. Save a new .zip backup instead.

Make a backup before changing phones or reinstalling.

Announcements

The bell in the Documents top bar shows news and update notices, with a badge for unread items. Open it to filter by All, Info or Update, and Mark all read. If a newer version exists, a dialog offers Update (opens the store page) or Later; you can keep working. OwlMD fetches announcements from the network only for this, at most once a day. Without a connection you see what was fetched before.

Tips

The first time you reach the Documents tab, the Search tab or the editor, a short tip card points at a control and explains it: a ring marks the control and an arrow on the card points to it. There are six tips in all. Tap Next (or anywhere on the dimmed screen) for the next one, Skip all to hide the rest of that screen's tips for good, and Done on the last one. The highlighted control does nothing while a tip is showing. A tip never closes the keyboard: if the keyboard would cover what it points at, the tip waits until you close it.

To see the tips again, open Settings → Help and feedback → Show tips again.

Settings

The Settings tab is in groups. Every path in this guide starts from here.

Appearance and language

  • Theme: System, Cairo, Cairo Night or Sepia.
  • Language: English, العربية, Bahasa Indonesia, or Use my device's language. It changes at once.
  • Reduce transparency: solid bars instead of frosted glass.
  • Hide bar while scrolling: the bottom bar slides away as you scroll down.

Reading and library

  • Manage tags and Trash.
  • Keep screen awake while reading: only while a note is open.

Privacy and data

  • Backup: opens the sheet described under Backup and restore.
  • App lock: asks for your fingerprint or device PIN to open OwlMD. When it is on, it also shows Lock after and Hide in recents & block screenshots.

Help and feedback

  • Help and user guide: this guide.
  • Send feedback: opens a sheet with Report a Bug and Request a Feature. Each opens your email app with a message ready to send. The same two actions, and Get Help, are at the end of this guide under "Still need help?".
  • Show tips again.

About

  • Version: touch and hold it to copy the number.
  • Privacy policy: opens the policy in your browser.
  • Open-source licences: the fonts and libraries OwlMD uses.
  • Diagnostics: errors are logged on your device only (no notes, titles or file paths). Nothing is sent automatically; Send diagnostic report opens the share sheet, and Clear log deletes the log.
