Also inside

  • Callouts

    Callouts in 14 types, and highlight.

  • Tables

    Tables, including right-to-left tables.

  • Images

    From your gallery, camera or a web link, or pasted in.

  • Folders

    Folders inside folders, sorted by date, name or length. Trash keeps deleted notes and folders for 7 days.

  • Print and share

    Printable PDF: black and white, no tinted fills. Share a note as a Markdown file, or open .md files.

  • Open and learn

    Open Markdown from a GitHub link, and a built-in guide. The app speaks English, Arabic and Indonesian.
